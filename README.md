# AI BA Copilot

An independent portfolio project exploring how a Business Analyst embedded within an AI & Innovation function can turn unstructured business pain points into evidenced, prioritised and measurable opportunities.

> Status: Discovery

The project is designed as a realistic mini industry project covering Business Analysis, AI opportunity assessment, requirements engineering, value measurement, governance and controlled AI-assisted workflows.

All organisational scenarios and operational data used for demonstration are synthetic. The project does not represent the internal processes or systems of any specific organisation.
