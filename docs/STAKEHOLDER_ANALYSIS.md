# Stakeholder Analysis

Status: DRAFT
Owner: Namit Singh
Last Updated: 2026-09-11
Phase: 1 — Formal Discovery & Business Case

> DRAFT stakeholder model for a hypothesised AI & Innovation function. No named individuals — synthetic roles only. RACI is not finalised at this stage.

## Stakeholder categories

| ID | Stakeholder | Interest | Influence | Decision responsibility | Information needs | Potential concerns |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| STK-01 | Business stakeholder (requestor) | Their pain point gets solved | Medium | Owns the business problem; accepts/limited on solution | Whether/when their problem is addressed; expected value | Being ignored; slow process; jargon |
| STK-02 | Business Analyst | Runs discovery; quality of evidence & requirements | Medium | Owns discovery, evidence, requirements, traceability | Structured intake; evidence; stakeholder input; governance fields | Tool adds overhead; AI output unreliable; accountability |
| STK-03 | Head of AI & Innovation | Portfolio value; prioritisation; risk | High | Prioritises; funds; go/no-go | Prioritised, evidenced opportunities; portfolio view | Wasted spend; unclear value; governance exposure |
| STK-04 | Technical / Engineering | Technical feasibility & effort | Medium-High | Assesses feasibility; builds pilots | Clear problem, data availability, constraints | Vague requirements; scope creep; premature solutioning |
| STK-05 | Finance | Cost, benefit, ROI | Medium-High | Approves spend; validates benefit case | Cost categories; baselines; expected-vs-actual value | Unquantified/fabricated ROI; runaway AI costs |
| STK-06 | Data / Privacy / Governance | Lawful, governed AI use | High (gate) | DPIA/governance sign-off | Personal-data use; DPIA triggers; oversight; fairness [EV-004][EV-005][EV-006] | Governance considered late; unauditable decisions |
| STK-07 | Delivery / Product | Turning opportunities into delivery | Medium | Owns delivery once approved | Requirements, acceptance criteria, priority | Poorly framed handover; missing acceptance criteria |
| STK-08 | End users | Usable, adopted solutions | Low-Medium | Adopt or reject in practice | Fit to real work; training | Low usability; imposed tools; no adoption support |

## Notes

- STK-06 acts as a governance gate; the workflow should capture governance fields early, not at the end.
- STK-08 (end users) matter to the value gap: deployment ≠ adoption ≠ value [EV-002][EV-003].
- Interest/influence ratings are DRAFT judgements from secondary research, to be validated against synthetic stakeholder scenarios in later discovery.
