## Proposed lifecycle and challenge

Use six decision stages, with parallel stakeholder engagement and assurance. Stage identifiers LC-001..006 identify this model's business stages; they are not Jira statuses, screens or approved product requirements. Combining discovery/stakeholders/evidence reduces serial handoffs; triage brings governance forward; feasibility/value/adoption run together so trade-offs can change options before ranking. A low-risk, well-evidenced request may use brief records for each relevant decision; bypassing paperwork does not bypass decision accountability.

## LC-001 — Receive, clarify and screen

- Why/decision: determine whether there is a business question worth examining, who owns it and whether immediate specialist escalation is needed.
- Required information: original request, affected people, intended decision/use, urgency, known harm/data sensitivity, existing case/reference and possible duplicate.
- Evidence produced: request-to-problem reference, named business owner, initial risk flags, unknowns and triage rationale.
- Participants/authority: STK-01 and STK-02, with STK-08 where needed; STK-03 owns prioritised analysis capacity, STK-06 owns specialist assurance disposition. BA recommends route but cannot grant data permission.
- Sufficient evidence/exit: owner and intended use clear enough to bound discovery, or explicit reason they are absent; risk flags recorded and specialist referral made where necessary. Unknown risk is not a passed gate.
- Routes: LC-002; REFINE back to requestor; MORE EVIDENCE REQUIRED with named information owner; PARK with review trigger; STOP by sponsor; ESCALATE GOVERNANCE for consequential/sensitive uncertainty. SCN-004 may pause here.

## LC-002 — Establish problem, people and current state

- Why/decision: determine what problem is credible, who experiences it and what causal explanations remain open.
- Required information: request record, current process, stakeholder roles, baseline/denominator/period, provenance, exceptions, data limitations, alternative explanations.
- Evidence produced: agreed-for-analysis problem statement, current-state map, evidence/gap ledger, stakeholder responsibility map and assumption links.
- Participants/authority: STK-02 leads; STK-01 confirms business context, STK-08 challenges work reality; STK-04/05/06 contribute when relevant. Business owner confirms need; BA owns analysis quality, not empirical truth.
- Sufficient evidence/exit: distinction between observed/stipulated fact, interpretation and unknown; enough evidence to compare plausible interventions. An unresolved material gap can support an evidence-gathering recommendation, not an unsupported benefit claim.
- Routes: LC-003; REFINE to LC-001/002; MORE EVIDENCE REQUIRED; PARK/STOP through sponsor if need is absent or not worth investigation; assurance escalation if new risk emerges.

## LC-003 — Compare AI, non-AI and no-change options

- Why/decision: select which plausible approaches merit assessment; avoid treating the requestor's AI idea as the answer.
- Required information: LC-002 problem/evidence, existing processes/tools, constraints, candidate benefits and harms, dependencies and non-AI alternatives.
- Evidence produced: options comparison with rejected alternatives and reasons; conditional shortlist, including do nothing and process/rules options.
- Participants/authority: BA facilitates; business owner and end users assess fit; engineering assesses technical plausibility; finance/governance contribute early. BA recommends shortlist; relevant specialists retain veto/assurance authority within their remit.
- Sufficient evidence/exit: at least one credible alternative to the favoured idea evaluated, with explicit uncertainties and rationale; no option selected solely for novelty.
- Routes: LC-004 for proportionate assurance; REFINE options/problem; MORE EVIDENCE REQUIRED; PARK/STOP if no worthwhile option. NON-AI ROUTE is a proposed destination, confirmed at LC-006 after relevant controls, not permission to bypass assurance.

## LC-004 — Assess feasibility, governance, value and adoption

- Why/decision: determine conditions under which an option could responsibly progress, and whether analysis cost is justified.
- Required information: shortlist; data readiness/access; technical feasibility judgement; costs/benefit drivers; current baseline; affected groups/decision effects; oversight; operational owner; change capacity and measurement plan.
- Evidence produced: distinct specialist dispositions, unresolved blockers, conditional value ranges, adoption/measurement ownership and sensitivity notes. Record who decided, scope, evidence/date and expiry/revisit trigger.
- Participants/authority: STK-04 technical assessment; STK-06 case-specific data/privacy/governance assurance; STK-05 financial validation/spend control; STK-01 operational benefit/change ownership; STK-07 delivery assessment; STK-02 integrates. Organisational delegations must be confirmed.
- Sufficient evidence/exit: hard gates passed for the action being requested, or a bounded recommendation to resolve blockers. Funding, lawful processing or delivery approval must never be inferred from BA approval. Distinguish approval to investigate from approval to implement.
- Routes: LC-005 for eligible options; REFINE to LC-003; MORE EVIDENCE REQUIRED; ESCALATE GOVERNANCE; PARK; STOP. A governance hold blocks the affected activity, not necessarily safe desk analysis or consideration of a different non-AI option.

## LC-005 — Prioritise eligible work and form recommendation

- Why/decision: compare justified next actions within limited capacity; decide what the BA recommends and why.
- Required information: LC-004 dispositions, comparable value/effort evidence, strategic relevance, opportunity cost, dependencies and competing demand.
- Evidence produced: transparent comparison, confidence labels, sensitivity/tie notes, recommendation and dissent. See PRIORITISATION_APPROACH.md.
- Participants/authority: BA owns recommendation; STK-03 owns portfolio priority under delegated authority; finance, business owner, engineering, governance and delivery inform it.
- Sufficient evidence/exit: incomparable or weak estimates visible; blocked options not promoted by attractive scores; next action, owner, conditions and review trigger stated. Without a real portfolio, no claim of optimal ranking.
- Routes: LC-006; REFINE; MORE EVIDENCE REQUIRED; PARK if capacity/dependency prevents action. Fresh risks return to LC-004.

## LC-006 — Accountable decision and handoff

- Why/decision: obtain a recorded authorised disposition and ensure someone owns the next work and eventual measurement.
- Required information: recommendation, evidence links, specialist conditions, resource authority, dissent and proposed next-stage measurement/change owners.
- Evidence produced: decision disposition, accountable decision-maker, date, rationale, conditions, receiving owner acknowledgement, unresolved gaps and review trigger. No fabricated signatures or approvals.
- Participants/authority: STK-03 for portfolio/go-no-go within delegation; STK-05 for spend; STK-06 for assurance; STK-04 for technical judgement; STK-01 for operational ownership; STK-07 for accepting delivery handoff. BA records and recommends. Project owner approves this portfolio project's substantive drafts.
- Sufficient evidence/exit: each required authority's disposition explicit, scope of permission stated and receiving owner identified; otherwise decision remains pending.
- Routes: PROCEED to an explicitly authorised later phase; NON-AI ROUTE to responsible process owner; REFINE to identified earlier stage; MORE EVIDENCE REQUIRED with owner/question; PARK with revisit date/event; STOP with rationale. ESCALATE GOVERNANCE returns to LC-004 when specialist decision unresolved.

## Route discipline

PARK retains a viable question but lacks priority, readiness or evidence-access now; STOP closes the current proposition for a stated reason. Neither deletes the record. Reopen only when the recorded trigger changes. MORE EVIDENCE REQUIRED names what would change the decision and a proportionate way to obtain it. REFINE changes the proposition; it is not a request to manufacture supportive evidence. PROCEED is bounded to the authorised next action, never blanket permission to build.

No real opportunity is approved by this operating-model proposal. Phase 2 ends with a decision-ready operating-model proposal; Phase 3 requires separate owner authorisation.
