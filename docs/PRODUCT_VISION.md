# Product Vision

Status: DRAFT
Owner: Namit Singh
Last Updated: 2026-09-11
Phase: 1 — Formal Discovery & Business Case

## Problem
Unstructured AI demand is qualified inconsistently and rarely measured, contributing to high abandonment and low realised value [EV-001][EV-002][EV-003].

## Target User
Primary: a Business Analyst embedded in an AI & Innovation function. Secondary stakeholders per docs/STAKEHOLDER_ANALYSIS.md.

## Vision
A BA-led workspace that carries a single business pain point end to end — discovery → stakeholders → evidence → opportunity → AI/non-AI options → research → value/ROI → BA recommendation → requirements → pilot → measurement → scale/iterate/stop — with AI assisting and the BA deciding. This journey is a HYPOTHESIS to validate, not a fixed requirement.

## Value Proposition
Turn business problems into evidenced, prioritised and measurable AI opportunities with traceability, responsible-AI governance capture and explicit human-approval gates — addressing the framing/evidence/governance/measurement gaps behind AI failure.

## Product Principles
- AI assists; the BA decides. AI output is never auto-approved [EV-007].
- Deterministic software owns calculations, rule validation, metrics and workflow state.
- Evidence and recommendation are shown together; assumptions are labelled.
- Provider independence; no vendor lock-in where avoidable.
- Governance and traceability are first-class, captured early.
- Measure the gap: deployed ≠ adopted ≠ value.

## Non-Goals
Autonomous AI consultant; AI making final business decisions; a generic chatbot interface; pretending to represent a real company's internal system; complexity for its own sake.
