## Thesis
AI adoption is widespread, while enterprise value realisation remains difficult; evidence points to adoption, integration, governance, measurement, value-definition and operating-model challenges [EV-002][EV-011][EV-012]. AI BA Copilot tests whether a BA-led operating workflow can provide connective discipline from business problem through governed, measured outcome. This mechanism is a hypothesis, not a proven fact.

## Target User
Primary: a Business Analyst embedded in an AI & Innovation function. Secondary stakeholders per docs/STAKEHOLDER_ANALYSIS.md.

## Vision
A BA-led workspace that carries a single business request end to end — discovery → stakeholders → evidence → opportunity → AI/non-AI options → research → value/ROI → BA recommendation → requirements → pilot → measurement → scale/iterate/stop — with AI assisting and the BA deciding. The journey is a hypothesis to validate, not a fixed requirement.

## Value Proposition (hypothesised)
If validated, turn business requests into evidenced, prioritised and measurable AI opportunities with traceability, responsible-AI governance capture and explicit human-approval gates.

## Product Principles
- AI assists; the BA decides. AI output is never auto-approved [EV-007].
- Deterministic software owns calculations, rule validation, metrics and workflow state.
- Evidence and recommendation shown together; assumptions labelled.
- Provider independence; no vendor lock-in where avoidable.
- Governance and traceability first-class, captured early (reflecting ICO/DUAA and the UK Gov AI Playbook practice principles [EV-013][EV-014]).
- Measure the gap: deployed ≠ adopted ≠ value.

## Non-Goals
Autonomous AI consultant; AI making final business decisions; a generic chatbot interface; representing any real company's internal system; complexity for its own sake.
