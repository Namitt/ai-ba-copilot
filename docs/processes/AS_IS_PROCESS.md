> **Hypothesised generic current state derived from secondary research, to be validated against future stakeholder scenarios.** This is NOT an observed process of any specific organisation. Every step and every failure mode below is a HYPOTHESIS / POTENTIAL FAILURE MODE, not an observation presented as reality.

## Hypothesised current-state flow (to validate)

1. **Intake (hypothesised):** a business request ("Can AI solve this?") arrives via meeting, message, idea or complaint.
2. **Triage (hypothesised):** an innovation lead or engineer forms an early view.
3. **Solutioning (hypothesised):** a tool/model may be considered before the problem, evidence and non-AI options are examined.
4. **Evidence (hypothesised):** baseline data may be limited; value may be asserted rather than measured.
5. **Prioritisation (hypothesised):** criteria may be inconsistent; portfolio view partial.
6. **Governance (hypothesised):** data-protection/DPIA/oversight may be considered late or informally [EV-004][EV-005][EV-013].
7. **Pilot (hypothesised):** a PoC is built; success criteria may be vague.
8. **Measurement (hypothesised):** deployment may be treated as success; adoption and value may not be measured against a baseline (deployed ≠ adopted ≠ value) [EV-002][EV-012].
9. **Outcome (evidenced at aggregate level):** across organisations, a large share of AI activity shows no measurable enterprise value [EV-002][EV-011][EV-012] — though the *causal path* above is hypothesised, not observed here.

## Validation note

None of the steps or failure modes is claimed as fact. Before any TO-BE design they must be tested against synthetic stakeholder scenarios (and, if available, practitioner input). Only the aggregate outcome (the adoption-vs-value gap) is evidence-supported; the specific process and its failure modes are the project's hypothesis.
