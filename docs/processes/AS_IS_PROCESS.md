# AS-IS Process (Hypothesised)

Status: DRAFT
Owner: Namit Singh
Last Updated: 2026-09-11
Phase: 1 — Formal Discovery & Business Case

> **Hypothesised generic current state derived from secondary research, to be validated against future stakeholder scenarios.** This is NOT an observed process of any specific organisation. It illustrates common failure points reported in the evidence [EV-001][EV-002][EV-003].

## Hypothesised current-state flow

1. **Unstructured intake** — a business request ("Can AI solve this?") arrives via meeting, message, idea or complaint. Often no standard capture.
2. **Ad-hoc triage** — an innovation lead or engineer forms an early opinion; problem framing and stakeholders are not systematically captured.
3. **Premature solutioning** — a tool/model is often assumed before the problem, evidence and non-AI options are examined.
4. **Thin evidence** — limited baseline data; value is asserted, not measured.
5. **Prioritisation by intuition or loudest voice** — inconsistent criteria; portfolio view partial.
6. **Late governance** — data protection / DPIA / oversight considered late or informally [EV-004][EV-005][EV-006].
7. **Pilot** — a PoC is built; success criteria may be vague.
8. **Weak measurement** — deployment is treated as success; adoption and business value are rarely measured against a baseline (deployed ≠ adopted ≠ value) [EV-002][EV-003].
9. **Stall or abandon** — a large share is abandoned after PoC or never reaches P&L impact [EV-001][EV-002].

## Validation note

Each step and pain point is a hypothesis. Before any TO-BE design, these must be tested against synthetic stakeholder scenarios (and, if ever available, real practitioner input). No claim is made that this reflects a specific organisation.
