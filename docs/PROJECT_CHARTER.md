## Project
AI BA Copilot

## Thesis
AI adoption is widespread, while enterprise value realisation remains difficult. Evidence points to adoption, integration, governance, measurement, value definition and operating-model challenges [EV-002][EV-011][EV-012]. AI BA Copilot tests whether a BA-led operating workflow can provide connective discipline from business problem through governed, measured outcome. **This causal mechanism is not claimed to be proven; it is the project's hypothesis.**

## Purpose
Validate (or challenge) that hypothesis through structured, evidenced discovery, with AI assisting and a human BA deciding.

## Business Problem
The evidenced problem is the adoption-vs-value gap. Whether inconsistent BA-style discovery materially contributes is a hypothesis (ASM-010, OPEN/UNVALIDATED).

## Objectives
- Test the problem and the core journey against synthetic stakeholder scenarios.
- Produce evidenced, traceable discovery artefacts.
- Define a value/measurement method (baseline → expected → actual).
- Keep governance and human-approval explicit.

## Scope
Phase 1 discovery and synthesis; synthetic scenarios only.

## Out of Scope
Technology stack, application build, detailed functional requirements, feature backlog, synthetic application data, real-company data — deferred.
