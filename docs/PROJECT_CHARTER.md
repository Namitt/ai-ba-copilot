## Project
AI BA Copilot

## Purpose
Demonstrate and validate a BA-led operating workflow that turns unstructured business pain points into evidenced, prioritised and measurable AI (or non-AI) opportunities, with AI assisting and a human BA deciding.

## Business Problem
AI/innovation functions receive unstructured demand and progress it without consistent discovery, evidence, prioritisation, governance or value measurement, contributing to high abandonment and low realised value [EV-001][EV-002][EV-003].

## Objectives
- Validate the problem and the core journey against synthetic stakeholder scenarios.
- Produce evidenced, traceable discovery artefacts (business case, problem, stakeholders, AS-IS, assumptions, risks).
- Define a value/measurement method (baseline → expected → actual).
- Keep governance and human-approval explicit throughout.

## Scope
Phase 1 discovery and synthesis: research, problem/business case, stakeholder model, hypothesised AS-IS, assumptions and risks; Jira/Notion/GitHub records; synthetic scenarios only.

## Out of Scope
Technology stack selection, application build, detailed functional requirements, product feature backlog, synthetic application data, and any real-company data — all deferred to later phases.

## Constraints
Free-first tooling; provider independence; synthetic data only; no implication of real internal company data; human approval for material AI outputs; defensible in interview; traceability and governance required.

## Success Criteria
A credible, evidenced, owner-reviewable discovery baseline that either supports proceeding to deeper discovery or honestly reports that the concept is not sufficiently differentiated. No fabricated metrics.
