## Convention and authority limits

R = performs/co-ordinates the work; A = accountable for this specific activity/decision; C = consulted; I = informed; — = not routinely involved. A/R combines accountability and performance. One A per row is a modelling convention, not a claim about every organisation's legal or financial delegations. Validate actual delegations before use. The project owner alone approves these substantive project drafts.

The broad Phase 1 role STK-01 is used here as business problem/operational owner only when that authority is explicitly delegated; a requestor without it must nominate an authorised owner. STK-03 portfolio authority does not replace STK-05 spend authority. STK-06 is a grouped function: identify the specific competent decision-maker and any independent review required. STK-08 includes affected end users; consultation is not accountability for programme success.

## Escalation and scenario challenge

- SCN-001: STK-01 must own queue capacity/adoption, not merely delegate success to BA or model accuracy. Finance separates capacity from cash value.
- SCN-002: finance retains approval-policy/spend authority; a rules proposal does not authorise purchases or change permissions.
- SCN-003: specialist assurance and accountable case decisions cannot be replaced by sponsor urgency or BA recommendation. The operational case decision-maker is a delegated role within the service-owner model, not an AI agent. Actual role allocation remains to be established in a real organisation.
- SCN-004: absent operational decision owner is a reason to clarify/park, not to assign the BA permanent accountability.

If authorities disagree, record the conflict, affected action and escalation owner. Do not mark the gate passed until the required authorised disposition exists. Accountabilities for portfolio priority, financial permission, assurance and delivery are separate even if one person holds several roles.
