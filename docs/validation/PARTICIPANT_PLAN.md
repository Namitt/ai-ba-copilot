# Track A Participant Plan

Status: APPROVED
Owner: Namit Singh
Phase: 3.5 — Pre-Build Validation

> **APPROVED — Phase 3.5 validation-design baseline (2026-09-14; DEC-013).** Approval followed owner CONDITIONAL PASS, amendment cycles and a final consistency/terminology cleanup; it authorises this design as the current baseline and does **NOT** authorise recruitment, participant execution, the dry run, Track B, Phase 4 or a build. Defines participant profile, practical target, briefing and data handling for Track A. **No participants are fabricated and no recruitment has occurred.** Purpose is directional validation, not population-level inference.

## 1. Participant profile

The intended user of the discipline is a **Business Analyst**, so the sample must be **BA-heavy**: the **majority must be practising BAs** (or clearly BA-equivalent hands-on roles). Adjacent roles **supplement** but must **not dominate**:
- **Business Analysts (majority)** — the core profile under test
- Product / Change professionals (supplementary)
- AI / Innovation professionals (supplementary)
- Data / Transformation professionals (supplementary)

Adjacent roles are included to see whether the discipline travels across profiles, but a result driven mainly by non-BAs would not answer the question, so recruitment prioritises practising BAs. No requirement for AI expertise.

## 2. Practical validation target (not a statistical sample)

A **practical directional target of 6–8 *completed* participants** is proposed (i.e. 6–8 who finish valid sessions, excluding the dry run) — enough to surface patterns and usability problems, explicitly **not** a statistically justified sample size. The **majority must be practising BAs** (§1). Labelled as a practical validation target; conclusions will be directional. The owner may adjust. Recruitment is not claimed and not started.

## 3. Briefing (neutral; avoids revealing the desired answer)

- Explain the task ("work these opportunity cases as you normally would / using this template") without stating what "good" looks like or that a particular route is expected.
- Do not tell participants the study hopes the structured approach wins.
- Facilitator does not coach mid-task or correct reasoning during a case.
- Counterbalance condition order across participants.

## 4. Data handling & anonymisation

- Record only: an anonymised participant ID (e.g. P1, P2…), a broad role class (from §1), the structural observations, artefacts produced, time/effort, and subjective feedback.
- Do **not** collect unnecessary personal information (no full name, employer, contact details, or sensitive attributes in the study record).
- Store outputs under anonymised IDs; keep any name↔ID mapping (if one is even needed to schedule) separate from the study data and out of the repository.
- Nothing about real employers or confidential projects is recorded; scenarios are synthetic.

## 5. Briefing & consent protocol (lightweight, proportionate, fixed before sessions)

Fixed **before** any session so it is applied identically to everyone:
1. **Purpose stated neutrally** — "directional validation of a way of working; we are testing the process, not you." The hoped-for result is **not** announced.
2. **Voluntary & withdrawable** — participation is voluntary; the participant may pause or stop at any time and may decline any case.
3. **What is recorded** — anonymised ID, broad role class, their case outputs, time/effort, and short feedback; **no unnecessary personal data** (§4).
4. **Anonymisation & use** — outputs stored under an anonymised ID; used only for this directional validation; not attributed to them by name.
5. **Synthetic cases** — all scenarios are synthetic; participants are asked **not** to introduce real confidential employer information.
6. **Consent captured lightly** — a simple recorded verbal/written acknowledgement of the above before starting; no signature ceremony, but consent is explicit.
7. **Debrief** — a neutral debrief (including the actual aim) is offered afterwards; **raw outputs are preserved before any debrief interpretation** (bias control).

No deception is used beyond not pre-announcing the hoped-for result.

### 5a. Scoring/reviewer note
Controlled wording amendment (2026-09-14): the **participant-facing format generally reveals Condition A vs Condition B**, so **treatment blinding is NOT assumed** and no "blind-ish" claim is made. Accurate controls apply instead: outputs de-identified (pseudonymous participant ID); rubric and scenario expectations fixed before testing; raw outputs preserved; an evidence note required for each awarded indicator; scoring completed before viewing aggregate A-vs-B patterns where practical. Where practical, a **second reviewer scores a subset** of outputs and disagreements are recorded; **no inter-rater-reliability statistic is claimed unless it is actually measured**, and **no blinding is claimed unless condition-unlabelled material was actually provided to a reviewer** (SCORING_RUBRIC §6).

## 6. Boundaries

Directional only; small n; synthetic cases. Results will not be generalised to enterprise outcomes and will not be used to claim ASM-002/ASM-004/ASM-010 are validated.
